# AI ARENA — HARD MODE
8-Hour AIML Hackathon | 08:00 AM–03:00 PM

## Objective
Build an ML system that performs well on private hidden data, including distribution shifts and difficult/ambiguous cases.

## Allowed
AI assistants may be used for coding, debugging, documentation, research, and brainstorming.

## Not allowed
Do not access, infer, or share organizer-only files. Do not search for or use external copies of the private test set.

## Evaluation
The organizer evaluates predictions on a private hidden set. Validation performance is not the final score.

## Expected submission
Submit predictions in the exact format specified by your track README, plus your source code/notebook and a short README describing your approach.

## Important
A high validation score alone does not guarantee a high final score. Robustness to unseen conditions matters.
